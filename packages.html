Are there any hidden setup fees or ad spend percentages?

No hidden markups. We charge a flat, predictable monthly sprint fee. Ad spend is billed directly to your credit card by Google or Meta, so you keep 100% of your credit card points and complete billing clarity.

Yes. Because our work runs in agile 30-day sprint cycles, you can upgrade, scale up creative production, or adjust channel focus at the start of any new monthly cycle with zero penalties.

Yes. All UGC video scripting, creator procurement, editing, graphic variations, and animation are fully included within our Paid Media and Omnichannel packages without extra studio fees.

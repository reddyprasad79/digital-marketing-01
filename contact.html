Audit Preparation: 3 Simple Items To Prepare

To make your 30-minute growth diagnostic session maximally actionable, having these numbers handy allows us to uncover immediate ROAS optimizations:

Current Ad Spend & ROAS

Your last 30 to 90 days total ad budget across Google, Meta, or influencer channels, alongside your blended return on ad spend.

Target Acquisition Cost (CAC)

The maximum allowable cost per acquisition for your business model to remain profitable on first-order purchases or B2B contracts.

Current Top Funnel Assets

Links to your primary landing pages, best-performing creatives, and competitor brands you admire in your niche.

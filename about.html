Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Stackly's performance squads.

How do your 10-day sprint cycles work?

Every 10 days, we analyze creative hook performance, retire fatigued ads, deploy 16+ new variations, and re-allocate budget into top-performing audience angles to ensure sustained high ROAS.

Who owns the ad accounts and data?

You retain 100% ownership of your Meta, Google, TikTok, and analytics accounts forever. We never lock client pixels or creative assets in proprietary holding accounts.

What is your performance SLA guarantee?

If our campaigns do not hit mutually agreed-upon monthly growth targets, our senior strategy squad works completely free of charge during the subsequent month until baseline targets are restored.

How fast do we see results after onboarding?

Attribution calibration and initial ad campaigns go live within 5 to 7 business days. For paid media, initial lead and sales velocity is typically observable within the first 48 hours of campaign launch.
